## What's Growing

**Canopy / Large Shrub**
Banana, Moringa, Elderberry (12ft+), Mulberry, Fig, Loquat

**Shrub Layer**
Pigeon Pea, Katuk, Longevity Spinach, Beautyberry, Firebush

**Herbaceous**
Turmeric, Galangal, Ginger, Lemongrass, Comfrey, Tithonia

**Ground Cover**
Perennial Peanut, Sweet Potato, Okinawa Spinach

**Vines**
Passionflower, Coral Honeysuckle

**Fungi**
3 worm bins active. Mycelium zones under mulch in canopy layer.

## By the Numbers

- **Lawn replaced:** ~2,800 sq ft
- **Species planted:** 40+
- **Annual inputs now:** Occasional compost top-dress, rainwater
- **Harvest:** Bananas, figs, loquat, elderberries, turmeric, ginger, greens year-round
- **Time to low-maintenance:** ~18 months

---

## The Honest Part

**What worked better than expected:**
Moringa. Pigeon pea as a nitrogen source. Sheet mulching directly over grass with no removal.

**What failed or struggled:**
Anything that needed consistent moisture in the first summer before roots established. Lost several plants to the 2023 heat.

**What I'd do differently:**
Start with more comfrey earlier. The biomass production pays dividends faster than almost anything else.
