<h1>The Great American Lawn: How We Turned Nature Into a Green Buzzcut</h1>

Because nothing says “I love the Earth” like chemical warfare and mowing every Saturday.

Once upon a time, a bunch of European aristocrats decided grass was sexy.

Not wildflowers.
Not edible plants.
Just grass.

So we imported the idea, stripped the soil, banned dandelions, and invented an entire industry to keep it alive against its will.

Now we pour 3 trillion gallons of water a year on lifeless green carpets — while native species die ten feet away.

It’s not a yard.
It’s a status symbol with a sprinkler addiction.

What if your yard didn’t need to be tamed?
What if it could breathe again?
