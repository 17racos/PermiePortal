        <h1>Moringa: The Miracle Tree for Health and Sustainability</h1>
        <strong>Moringa oleifera</strong>, often called the "Miracle Tree," is a fast-growing, drought-resistant plant native to South Asia and Africa. It has been used for centuries in traditional medicine and is now gaining global recognition for its exceptional nutritional value, medicinal properties, and environmental benefits.

        <div class="image-container">
            <img src=""alt="Moringa leaves and powder">
        </div>

        <h2>Nutritional Profile 💪</h2>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Vitamins:</strong> High in Vitamin A, C, E, and B-complex</li>
            <li><strong>Minerals:</strong> Rich in calcium, iron, magnesium, potassium, and zinc</li>
            <li><strong>Protein:</strong> Contains all nine essential amino acids</li>
            <li><strong>Antioxidants:</strong> High levels of quercetin, chlorogenic acid, and beta-carotene</li>
            <li><strong>Healthy Fats:</strong> Seeds are rich in oleic acid, similar to olive oil</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>Health Benefits of Moringa</h2>

        <div class="card">
            <h3>1. Packed with Antioxidants & Anti-Inflammatory Properties 🔥</h3>
            Moringa leaves contain powerful antioxidants like quercetin and chlorogenic acid, which help reduce oxidative stress, neutralizing harmful free radicals in the body. Its natural anti-inflammatory properties help alleviate conditions like arthritis, asthma, and chronic pain.

        </div>

        <div class="card">
            <h3>2. Supports Heart Health ❤️</h3>
            <ul>
                <li>Lowers cholesterol and triglyceride levels</li>
                <li>Regulates blood pressure with high potassium content</li>
                <li>Improves circulation and cardiovascular function</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        <div class="card">
        <h3>3. Boosts the Immune System 🌿</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Contains seven times more Vitamin C than oranges</li>
            <li>Supports white blood cell production</li>
            <li>Antimicrobial properties combat bacteria and viruses</li>
        </ul>
        </div>
        <div class="card">
        <h3>4. Enhances Brain Function 🧠</h3>
        Moringa contains neuroprotective compounds that enhance memory, cognitive function, and mood stability. Its high antioxidant levels help protect neurons from oxidative damage, potentially reducing the risk of Alzheimer’s and dementia.

        </div>

        <div class="card">
        <h3>5. Supports Healthy Skin and Hair </h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Vitamin E and A promote healthy skin, reduce wrinkles, and fight acne</li>
            <li>Collagen production is enhanced by its high Vitamin C content/li>
            <li>Strengthens hair follicles, reducing hair loss and improving scalp health</li>
        </ul>
        </div>
        <div class="card">
        <h2>6. Improves Digestion & Gut Health 💪</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>Acts as a natural prebiotic, promoting good gut bacteria.</li>
            <li>Contains isothiocyanates, which help prevent ulcers and support stomach lining health.</li>
            <li>Anti-parasitic properties combat intestinal worms and infections.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>

        <div class="card">
            <h2>7. Natural Blood Sugar Regulator 💡</h2>

                Moringa has been studied for its antidiabetic effects, as it lowers blood sugar levels and improves insulin sensitivity.
                It helps prevent type 2 diabetes and can assist in blood sugar regulation after meals.

        </div>

        <div class="card">
            <h2>8. Detoxification & Liver Support 🌱</h2>
            <ul>
                <li>Moringa has hepatoprotective properties, meaning it protects the liver from toxins.</li>
                <li>Helps cleanse the body from heavy metals and environmental pollutants.</li>
                <li>Supports healthy kidney and liver function.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>

        <h2>How to Use Moringa</h2>
        <ul>
            <li><strong>Fresh Leaves:</strong> Eaten raw, in salads, or cooked like spinach.</li>
            <li><strong>Powder:</strong> Added to smoothies, teas, and soups.</li>
            <li><strong>Capsules:</strong> Convenient for supplementation.</li>
            <li><strong>Seeds:</strong> Used for water purification or eaten roasted.</li>
            <li><strong>Oil (Ben Oil):</strong> Applied for skin care and cooking.</li>
        </ul>
